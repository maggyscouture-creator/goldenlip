About GoldenLip

More Than
Just Makeup

Born in Nairobi, Kenya, GoldenLip Cosmetics was founded with a revolutionary idea: that luxury beauty should celebrate — not diminish — the natural beauty of African women.

Every shade in our collection is tested across a full spectrum of skin tones. Our formulas are developed with African climate conditions in mind — long-lasting, sweat-resistant, and breathtaking.

From our Nairobi headquarters, we now serve clients across Kenya, Africa, and the globe through our sister sites in the US and our dedicated skincare line.

Shipping Policy

Last updated: January 2024

At GoldenLip Cosmetics, we strive to deliver your orders swiftly and safely across Kenya and worldwide.

Nairobi Delivery

Orders within Nairobi are delivered within 1–2 business days. Free shipping on orders over KES 3,500.

Nationwide Shipping

We deliver to all 47 counties in Kenya via trusted courier partners. Standard delivery: 2–5 business days. Flat rate: KES 350.

International Shipping

We ship to East Africa, the rest of Africa, US, UK, and Europe. Rates and delivery times vary by destination.

M-Pesa Pay on Delivery

Pay via M-Pesa upon delivery for orders within Nairobi. Lipa Na M-Pesa available at checkout.

Returns Policy

Last updated: January 2024

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not completely happy, we'll make it right.

Return Window

You have 7 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return for unopened, unused products in their original packaging.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Opened cosmetics (for hygiene reasons)
  • Used brushes or applicators
  • Discounted or sale items

How to Return

Contact us via WhatsApp or email with your order number. We'll arrange collection or advise drop-off points in Nairobi.
